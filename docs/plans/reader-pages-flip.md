## 1. Invariants
1. The saved place stays sentence plus offset. A page number is never stored on the server or sent in a save.
2. The real section (`section[data-part]` in `#reader`) is always at the destination page the moment a turn starts. `firstShown`, read aloud, search and saving only ever look at it.
3. Everything drawn for a turn lives outside `#reader`, in one stage that is `aria-hidden`, `inert`, `pointer-events: none`, has no duplicate ids and no `hx-*`, and is gone when the turn ends. A turn never moves focus.
4. Faces are pixel-faithful: a face's text sits where the real page's text sits, within 1 px.
5. Hiding or showing the bar and dock never re-lays pages. Only size, look or the read-aloud strip do.
6. Forward within a look on one device, the number goes up by exactly 1 a page; the same page keeps its number; an estimate says "about".
7. CSP holds: CSS variables through `style.setProperty`, motion through `element.animate`. Never `setAttribute("style")`, `style.cssText` or `insertRule`.
8. Library files, bookmarks' meaning (a sentence id) and the server's place rules do not change.

## 2. Decisions
- **`auto` means pages at every width.** Asked by the operator. `wanted()` becomes `scroll` only for `scroll`. No migration: `parseReader` maps a stored `auto` to `pages`; `Validate` still accepts `auto` so an old sheet can post. The sheet offers Pages and Scroll. No-JavaScript stays the plain scroll page; the offline shell follows `wanted()`.
- **Measure in em, not ch.** 68ch is 879 px at 20 px in this face. Page text 30em (about 66 characters a line), least 24em. Scroll keeps its tokens.
- **A book on a desk from 1024 px; full bleed below.** Paper stays `--ground` so every contrast test still holds; the desk is a tint around it. Page height at most 1.6 x page width, snapped to whole lines.
- **Bar and dock: the desk absorbs them from 1024 px** (space reserved, fading leaves desk). **Below 1024 px they float over the page**, the page takes their height (about 3 more lines at 390), the reader opens with them hidden and a turn hides them, because a shown bar covers text. Resume never lands under a bar.
- **Numbers count the person's own pages**, per device and look, measured when a part is laid out, estimated for parts not seen. No background pagination: it would fetch and lay out a whole book on a phone. Printed labels stay as a second, smaller fact.
- **Faces are small slices, not whole-section clones.** Three copies of a 40,000-unit part would be three layouts at each crossing, stale after a highlight, and four times the DOM. A slice costs one page, is built fresh at each turn, and uses the same column engine, so break decisions match.
- **Rejected: View Transitions.** Two snapshots for four visible surfaces, the page freezes, and a drag cannot begin before the new state exists. **Rejected: canvas or `element()`** (not cross-engine, CSP-awkward).
- **Backface and 3D:** `.ts-flip__leaf` is `transform-style: preserve-3d`, overflow visible, no opacity or filter (each would flatten it). Faces are flat, `overflow: hidden`, `backface-visibility: hidden` plus `-webkit-` form. Verified here: WebKitGTK 2.52.6, Firefox 154. Guess until checked on a device: Android WebView at minSdk 28 only depends on an updated WebView, which `:has()` already needs.
- **`reader-flip.js` is lazy**; a person on Slide or None never loads it. Before it arrives, a turn slides.
- **The page edge is a real button**, so touch screen readers and keyboards get Next page and Previous page, and it is also the mouse's grab handle. Text selection with a mouse stays free.
- **A ribbon per physical page**, toggling a bookmark on that page's first sentence. The server does the toggle by sentence, so no bookmark id reaches the page.
- **Reduced motion gives None**, same as today.

## 8. Decision file text (`docs/decisions/0036-pages-by-default.md`)
> # 0036 Pages are the default, and a page has a number
> Date: 2026-11-09. Status: accepted when package A lands. Amends [0018](0018-book-reader.md) point 9.
> ## Context
> Books have pages. Scroll at 1280 px and up left people with no sense of progress and nothing to mark. Page numbers were hidden because a layout number is not a place.
> ## Decision
> 1. Pages at every width; `auto` reads as pages; a person who chose Scroll keeps it. No-JavaScript pages stay scroll.
> 2. A page is bounded (30em of text, 1.6 tall to wide). Two facing pages when both fit at 24em, from 1024 px.
> 3. The reader shows a page number that fits this device and look. It is display only, measured per part and kept on the device. Unseen parts are estimated from characters and the words say "about". The saved place stays a sentence and offset; a number is never stored on the server. The book's printed label stays as a second fact.
> 4. A bookmark stays a sentence id. A page is marked when a bookmarked sentence ends on it, at any size.
> 5. A turn is Flip (default), Slide or None, in `reader.v1`; reduced motion is None. The moving page is drawn from small `aria-hidden` slices outside `#reader`, and the real text is at its destination from the first frame.
> ## Not handled, and why
> Paginating a whole book ahead (a phone would fetch and lay out every part). Numbers shared between devices (they differ by screen). Running heads (a line of page for what the bar says).

The comment at `reader-pages.js` lines 3-4 becomes: "A page number here is for showing only: it fits this device and look and is kept on this device. The place that is saved is a sentence and an offset (reader.js, decision 0036)."

## 9. Out of scope, and limits named
Running heads. Page numbers in contents, marks sheet or skim tip. Merging short parts into one page. Curl deformation of the sheet (a flat leaf with shading is what CSS holds). Drag at a part edge on slow devices. Several bookmarks on one page: a press removes one, and the ribbon stays until none is left. A sentence longer than a page marks the page it ends on. Cross-device numbers. Exact pagination in the background. Right-to-left. Scroll flow: only `here` for the bar's bookmark changes. No edit to `tendshelf-handoff/`.

## 10. For the operator (not blocking; builders follow the recommendation)
1. Phone: the reader opens with the bar hidden and a turn hides it, in exchange for about 3 more lines and no bar over the text. Recommended: yes.
2. "Page 12 of about 340" until every part has been read, against an exact number that costs fetching and laying out the book on each new device or size. Recommended: "about".
3. A slightly tinted desk around white pages from 1024 px, against the same colour edge to edge with hairlines. Recommended: the desk, with no change to `contrast`.
