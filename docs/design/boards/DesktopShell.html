Desktop shell: our own title bar

Board for decision 0023. The app at its real size, with a Tendshelf title bar instead of the system's title and menu rows. Invented person and titles.

App icon

The handoff's icon, unchanged, at the sizes the desktop asks for. No new drawing. Below 32 px the wave fades into the ember square, so the tray uses the handoff's plain mark instead: cream on a dark panel, walnut on a light one.

The feel, for the words

  • A window that arrives ready. It stays hidden until the page has painted, on the theme's own ground, at the size it was left. No white flash and no splash screen.
  • Grab anywhere empty in the bar to move the window; double-click to maximise. Drag from the real window edge to resize.
  • Keep playing when I close the window is one line in the More menu, off by default, and offered only when the desktop shows a tray.
