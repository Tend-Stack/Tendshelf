A small popover for one note. The same note shows in the player's bookmarks list, because highlights, bookmarks and notes are shared across listening and reading.

- Opens from `.ts-notedot`; `role="dialog"` labelled with who wrote it; focus moves in, Esc closes and returns focus to the dot.
- The meta line always gives both places: chapter, audio time and page. Tapping the time plays from there.
- Notes are private to the person who wrote them unless they choose to share them with the household.
