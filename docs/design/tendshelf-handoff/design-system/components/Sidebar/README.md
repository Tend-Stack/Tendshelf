The desktop sidebar (1280px and up): wordmark, the main destinations with counts and shortcuts, the person's shelves, the admin section for owners, and the person at the bottom.

## Rules

- `size-sidebar` (264px) wide, `ground` background with a `line` rule on its right.
- The current item is `raised` with a 3px `accent` bar on its left edge and an `accent` icon.
- Counts and shortcut hints are `ink-subtle` and right-aligned; "2 pending" on Users & invites is the only count that is a call to action.
- The "Server" group appears only for owners and admins: Users & invites, Libraries, Metadata, and Research when it has been turned on.
- Research, when on, is listed last and opens into the lab's own cooler theme; nothing from the lab appears anywhere else in the sidebar.
- Keyboard: `/` focuses search, `G` then `L` goes to the Library, `G` then `H` to Home.
