A workbench for studying narration in books you own. Cut short clips, measure pace, pauses and pitch, and compare narrators.

  • Only files you own, already on this server. Copy-protected files are refused.
  • For analysis only. Clips can't be shared or played in the listening app.

You can turn it off any time. Clips are kept until you delete them.
